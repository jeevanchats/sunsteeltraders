# Prospect brief: WavOS (a product of WAVMART, Bengaluru)

## Business
WavOS is a cloud software product that runs a whole business in one place. It covers sales, operations, finance and people, and it is "Built for work that is made to order." Its main pitch is that it "starts from the customer PO and follows it to the last payment." The alternative it argues against is general ERPs that "make you join sales, purchase, stores and accounts yourself." It is sold to Indian businesses. It is "Built in Bengaluru for Indian business," supports GST, TDS, PF and ESI, and keeps data hosted in India. Pricing is a flat monthly fee by team size, from ₹2,999 a month, with a 14-day free trial. WavOS hasn't launched yet: this is a demo site, and several industry packs are marked "Coming soon."

## Audience
- **Who buys:** owners and managers of Indian made-to-order manufacturers with up to about 50 staff (the pricing tiers top out at "31–50 employees," and 50+ is "Talk to us"). The industry packs that are live now cover antennas and RF/defence electronics, electronics and control systems, electrical panels and switchgear, trading and distribution, and FMCG and process manufacturing.
- **What they care about:**
  - Finding material shortages before a build starts
  - Their real on-time delivery %
  - Customers who pay late
  - Margin on each order
  - Not typing the same order into several systems
  - Keeping their invoices in Tally or Saral
  - GST and payroll compliance
  - Pricing that isn't charged per user
- **Implied buyer:** likely the owner or director, since the site stresses "Approve from your phone" and that "Everyone on your team gets a login." The site doesn't name the buyer's role directly.

## Voice
- **Short, punchy and a bit cheeky:** "CRM? ERP? No. Just Business. WavOS." and "Business Runs Better with WavOS."
- **Plain verbs about results, not jargon:** "Build it, buy it, test it, ship it." "Get paid on time and know your margin." "Nothing ships untested."
- **Built around the buyer's problems:** "Catch shortages weeks early," "Know who pays late," "Stock you can trust."
- **Reassuring and practical:** "AI that does the typing … You check and confirm every one," "No per-user maths," "Live in days, and nothing to install."
- **Openly Indian:** "Made in India," "rupees throughout," and shop-floor terms like "job work at vendors," "AMC," "FAT," "burn-in."

## Gaps
- **No blog or resources section,** so nothing on the site can rank for searches like "manufacturing ERP for MSMEs" or "keep Tally." A competitor, TranZact, already publishes a 2026 guide to the "Best Manufacturing ERP in India for MSMEs." The guide recommends ready-made software that keeps Tally for accounts, which is also WavOS's own position.
- **The flat-price advantage isn't spelled out with numbers.** The site says "No per-user maths," but there's no comparison. One market roundup (elitemindz.co) puts typical SME ERP subscriptions at ₹500–2,000 per user per month, which makes the gap easy to show.
- **No proof yet:** no customer stories, logos, testimonials or numbers. That's expected before launch, but there's also no pilot or early-access story to fill the space.
- **The best ideas get one line each.** The order-by date formula ("needed-by minus lead time minus buffer"), the A–D payment rating and the QC gate on dispatch are only mentioned in passing.
- **Industry packs are just stage lists.** Ten of the fifteen are "Coming soon," and none has its own explanation or a "who it's for" section on the saved page. There's also no visible contact email or phone number.

1. "One connected platform for sales, operations, finance and people. From the first enquiry to the final delivery and payment."
2. "Order-by dates for every material — needed-by minus lead time minus buffer — flag late items long before the build starts. Multi-level BOMs and job work at vendors included."

## Opportunities
1. **"Order-by dates: how to catch material shortages weeks before the build"** (blog post plus a LinkedIn post with the formula as a graphic). It turns WavOS's most distinctive feature ("needed-by minus lead time minus buffer") into helpful content people can search for, which the site doesn't have yet.
2. **"What a 30-person team pays: flat price vs per-user ERP"** (landing-page section for the pricing page). It shows the "No per-user maths" claim in numbers, using the ₹8,999/mo Growth tier against the per-user market rate quoted by a third party (cite that source, don't present it as WavOS's figure).
3. **"One flow, your industry": one post per live industry pack** (a LinkedIn carousel series of 5 posts, each able to reuse clips from the launch teaser videos). Each pack already has a specific stage list (e.g. "Burn-in & test," "FAT," "QC release") that will feel familiar to buyers in that trade, and right now that material only exists as plain text lists.
